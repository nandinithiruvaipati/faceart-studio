Real-time AI face tracking

Your Face, Your Canvas

Turn on your camera and draw glasses, crowns, moustaches and freehand art that follow every move of your face. Everything runs privately in your browser.

Features

👓 Glasses

Frames locked to your eyes, scaled and rotated with your head.

👑 Crown

A golden crown that sits above your forehead.

🥸 Moustache

Placed under the nose, moves with your face.

✨ Decorations

Cheek blush, face-outline glow and sparkles.

🖌️ Freehand

Draw with mouse or finger. Colour, brush size and eraser included.

🔒 Private

No uploads, no storage, no accounts. Camera stops when you leave.

How it works

  1. 1Press Start Creating and allow camera access.
  2. 2MediaPipe Face Landmarker finds 478 facial points locally.
  3. 3Pick tools. Designs are drawn on a canvas and follow your face.

About

FaceArt is an open-source portfolio project built with Vite, vanilla JavaScript, the Canvas API and Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker. Video frames never leave your device.
